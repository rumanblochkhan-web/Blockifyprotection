1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to Blockify ("we", "our", or "us"). Blockify is an Android digital wellness application designed to help individuals overcome adult content distractions, build productive habits, maintain focus streaks, and guard their screen time through mindful interventions.

Zero-Surveillance Core Principle: Blockify was built from the ground up with a 100% on-device privacy guarantee. We do not monitor your private life, we do not log your personal messages, we do not record keystrokes, and we never sell your personal data to third parties.

This Privacy Policy complies strictly with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data Policy, Prominent Disclosure & Consent requirements, and the Account Deletion policy.

2. AccessibilityService API Disclosure (Mandatory Notice)

⚠️ Prominent Disclosure Regarding AccessibilityService API

In accordance with the Google Play Accessibility API Policy, this application requires the use of the Android AccessibilityService API to perform its core advertised functionality: blocking adult web content, unsafe applications, and explicit keywords.

Specifically, Blockify uses the AccessibilityService API for:

  • Real-Time Screen & URL Scanning: Detecting when a browser address bar or app window displays known adult domains, explicit keyword queries, or restricted package names that the user voluntarily configured for protection.
  • Immediate Focus Intervention: Instantly closing or redirecting the user away from harmful or triggering adult content to provide a mindful cooldown screen.
  • Anti-Tamper & Shield Protection: Detecting attempts to impulsively force-stop or disable protection during an active streak or focus lock window.

What the AccessibilityService DOES NOT DO:

  • ❌ It DOES NOT read, collect, or store private chat messages, WhatsApp conversations, emails, or personal documents.
  • ❌ It DOES NOT capture or log keystrokes, credit card numbers, passwords, or personal credentials.
  • ❌ It DOES NOT record audio, capture screenshots, or take background camera photos.
  • ❌ It NEVER transmits your browsing history or visited URLs to any external cloud server. All evaluation is performed 100% on-device in volatile device memory and discarded immediately.

The AccessibilityService is completely voluntary. It is activated only when you explicitly enable it in your Android Accessibility Settings and can be deactivated by you at any time in accordance with your configured focus cooldown settings.

3. Device Administrator Permission Disclosure

Blockify optionally requests the Android BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN permission.

  • Purpose: To prevent impulsive and immediate uninstallation of Blockify during high-urge vulnerability moments or active focus sessions.
  • Scope: This permission is used exclusively for the uninstall prevention shield. Blockify does NOT have the ability to remotely wipe your phone, lock your screen with a remote PIN, or change your device security credentials.
  • User Control: You can revoke Device Administrator privileges at any time through the in-app Emergency Stop feature or via Android Settings > Security > Device Admin Apps.

4. Draw Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Blockify uses the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission (Display Over Other Apps) solely to present the full-screen Shield Intervention Screen when an explicit adult website or blocked application is detected. This provides a breathing countdown, motivational quote, and immediate exit route to safeguard your recovery.

6. Authentication & Firebase Services

Blockify utilizes Google Firebase (a subsidiary of Google LLC) to manage secure user authentication and cloud synchronization:

  • Firebase Authentication: Allows users to securely sign in using their Google Account or Email/Password. Passwords are never stored in plaintext and are handled directly by Google's secure cryptographic infrastructure.
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores user streak statistics, protected day counts, and account tier information. All access is protected by strict zero-trust Firestore Security Rules requiring authenticated user token validation.

7. Google AdMob (Rewarded Video Ads)

Blockify offers users the option to watch voluntary Google AdMob Rewarded Video Ads in exchange for emergency unblock tokens or focus session credits:

  • AdMob uses the Android Advertising ID (AAID) to serve ads in compliance with the Google Play Developer Policy.
  • Users can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • We do not collect sensitive user recovery details or share browsing habits with advertising partners.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Requirement, you have full control over your personal data:

  • Retention: Your account profile, streaks, and focus metrics are retained as long as your account remains active.
  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time from Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  • Web / Email Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request complete erasure of your account and cloud data by emailing us at rumanblochkhan@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request - Blockify". Your request will be fulfilled within 7 business days.
